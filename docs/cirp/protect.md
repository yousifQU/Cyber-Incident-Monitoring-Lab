# CSF: Protect

Protect reduces the likelihood and impact of incidents before they happen, across both IT and OT.

**Controls called for in the CIRP**
- Strong access control and **least privilege** enforcement
- **Multi-factor authentication**
- Data handling standards
- Employee cybersecurity training
- Baseline protections that shrink the attack surface and make later response easier

**Why it matters for an energy provider:** weak safeguards can lead to power outages, compromised servers, regulatory violations, loss of public trust, and financial loss.

> **Lab tie-in:** Incident 2 showed what happens when Protect controls lag: `user-a` held `sudo` rights it did not need, shared and stale passwords were never rotated, and no MFA stood in front of SSH or RDP.
